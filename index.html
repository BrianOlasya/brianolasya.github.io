01 / INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION

Chen Hsong injection moulding machine — control system rebuild

Vetra Limited · January 2026 · Controls, hydraulics, firmware

Vickers DG4V-5-2AJ-M-U-H6-20 directional valves were traced and function-tested, and the RKC barrel temperature controllers verified. The contactor and terminal cabinet was stripped and rewired to a documented state. That made the machine safe to power, but it did not run: the control board was the fault, and no replacement part was available.

The I/O map was reconstructed from the machine's original manual, annotated by hand, and a replacement board etched in-house. Firmware is a five-state machine — idle, automatic valve cycling, manual from the interface, manual from hardware buttons, and heaters — with a solenoid interlock that prevents both directional relays being energised together under any state. Heater control runs two MAX6675 thermocouple channels driving solid-state relays, with a fault flag per channel. Any state change drops all outputs before the new state is applied.

The point was not only to make the machine run. A control board designed here can be changed at will — new cycle logic, new interlocks, new instrumentation — without waiting on a vendor abroad. The same workshop holds a Chen-Ho milling machine still on its original Anilam control, queued for a LinuxCNC retrofit on the same reasoning.

Context
Hydraulic injection moulding machine out of service, original control board obsolete and unobtainable. The hydraulics and mechanics were sound; only the controls were dead.
Work
Hydraulic circuit traced and tested; cabinet rewired; I/O map reconstructed from original documentation; replacement controller designed, etched and programmed.
Status
Machine returned to service under a control board owned and modifiable in-house, with no dependency on an overseas supplier. 740 lines of firmware, January 2026.
02 / MANUFACTURING

Injection moulding machine, built in-house

Vetra Limited · Nov 2024 – present · Machine design, fabrication, process

Three generations. The first is fully manual — a hand lever over a red band-heated barrel. The second is the machine that has produced most of the parts on this site: heavier steel, a three-phase motor, semi-automatic operation. The third is in CAD now — single-phase and fully automatic, so it runs on an ordinary supply.

The torpedo and nozzle were turned in-house and barrel temperature runs on thermocouple feedback.

Process control is by shot weight, checked on a bench scale each cycle against a reference part. Feedstock is virgin pellet where the part requires it and shredded recycled flake otherwise, which is what makes short runs viable at this scale.

Context
Small-batch plastic parts carry minimum order quantities and lead times a prototype schedule cannot absorb.
Work
Three machines designed and fabricated: manual lever, then three-phase semi-automatic, then a single-phase fully automatic build in progress.
Status
Generations one and two in service; generation three in design.

04 / CLEAN COOKING · IoT

PowerPay cooker controllers — EPC v1.1

GIVE Limited · Feb 2024 – present · Lead IoT hardware & manufacturing engineer

The board replaces the OEM control inside a standard 1000 W electric pressure cooker, so the mechanical envelope was fixed before schematic capture. Mains energy metering and relay switching share the board with a non-isolated supply, which sets the creepage and clearance requirements.

Boards are placed and reflowed on the GIVE / PowerPay Africa IoT surface-mount line, then hand-finished and tested at the assembly bench. A scorched control plate in February 2025 led to changes in heat path and clearance on the following revision.

Context
Electric pressure cookers and induction hobs need telemetry for carbon-credit tracking and prepaid control, within a household price point.
Work
Controller designed to the existing appliance envelope: ESP32, mains energy metering, relay switching, non-isolated supply. Assembled in-house.
Status
In production. First cooker retrofits date from July 2023; hardware cost reduced by over 50% against the previous design through BOM and workflow changes.

10 / PRECISION RETROFIT

Digital readout for an EDM, built from scratch

Independent · April 2026 · Metrology, firmware, electronics repair

Glass linear scales were fitted to the machine and the interface looms rebuilt — scale mounting, read-head alignment and DB9 termination. Counting runs on the ESP32 hardware pulse-counter peripheral rather than software polling, so no quadrature edges are lost at speed, with 64-bit shadow counters maintained by an overflow ISR in IRAM. The 5 V encoder outputs are level-shifted to 3.3 V on all six lines.

On top of the position readout the firmware carries the EDM-specific functions — target depth, electrode wear compensation, touch-off, step and retract, hold and alarm — with settings persisted in NVS and a web interface served from the device. The first phase was repairing the original commercial readout, built around a Xilinx XC9572XL CPLD driving seven-segment displays, at component level. The second was building a replacement of my own — for the same reason as the moulding machine controller: a readout designed here can be repaired, extended and reproduced locally instead of re-imported.

Context
Manual machine tools lose usefulness when the position readout fails and the manufacturer no longer supports it.
Work
Two phases: repair the existing commercial readout at component level, then design and build a locally-owned replacement — ESP32, hardware quadrature counting, EDM depth and wear functions, NVS persistence, web interface.
Status
In service, reading to three decimal places. 513 lines of firmware, April 2026.

ASSISTIVE TECHNOLOGY · GALLIUM TECH

Knock Knock — alerting system for Deaf and hard-of-hearing residents

Gallium Tech Limited · 2021 – 2023 · Hardware, firmware, production

A knock at a door is an audible event, which makes an ordinary hostel room inaccessible. Knock Knock replaces it with a visual one: a sensor node detects the knock, a server node routes it, and an actuator node in the room signals the resident. Three separate boards, so a room can carry only what it needs and the system extends by adding nodes rather than replacing them.

It was prototyped through-hole and then laid out again for surface mount so it could be produced in quantity — the EasyEDA archive still holds both sets, sensor, actuator and server in each. It was installed in the disabled students' hostels at Kenyatta University, carried the AT Innovator Award and DIAR 2023, and the doorbell work that became iBell grew directly out of it.

Context
Knocking is inaccessible to Deaf and hard-of-hearing residents, and retrofitting a hostel cannot mean rewiring it.
Work
Three-node architecture — sensor, server, actuator. Through-hole prototype, then an SMD layout for production.
Status
Installed at Kenyatta University, disabled students' hostels. Gallium Tech's largest product.
Recognition
AT Innovator Award, Innovate Now Cohort 5.0, 2022 · DIAR Awards 1st runner-up, Inclusive Deployment of Technology, 2023
RENEWABLE ENERGY · UPANDE

Roslyn Solar Kit — a solar system you can teach from

Upande Limited · 2021 – 2022 · Hardware, enclosure, deployment

A working solar installation that is also a teaching aid. Panel, charge controller, battery, inverter and both DC and AC loads are laid out on a single board inside a lockable outdoor cabinet, wired as a real system rather than a simulation — and printed over the top with the schematic, a legend and coloured flow arrows, so a student can trace the energy path with a finger while the lamps are lit.

Earlier revisions were bare DIN-rail panels labelled by hand in tape. The final units went out to schools in weatherproof cabinets on stands. This was the largest project I did at Upande.

Context
Renewable-energy teaching in Kenyan schools, where a diagram on a board does not show a student what a charge controller does.
Work
System design, custom boards and sensors, cabinet and mounting, printed instructional overlay, field deployment and validation.
Status
Deployed to schools.
TRACKING · NEVEREST

Shule Yetu — school bus RFID tracking

Neverest LLC · 2020 – 2021 · Hardware, firmware, enclosure

A boarding record for school transport: each child taps an RFID card, the unit logs the event and reports it over GSM. Because a bus has no supply of its own, the device runs from 18650 cells and has to survive a full route on them.

The whole arc is on record. The first board was etched by hand and carries the mark Powered by Neverest Technologies. It went through several 3D-printed enclosure revisions before a finished unit with a rocker switch and external antenna — and four board revisions plus a peripheral board survive in the EasyEDA archive, with three device firmwares and a response server in the sketch archive.

Context
Schools need to know which child boarded which bus, on a vehicle with no power, no network and no mounting provision.
Work
RFID-RC522 reader, GSM reporting, 18650 supply. Hand-etched prototype through four board revisions; enclosure iterated in 3D print.
Status
Built and enclosed. Four board revisions, three device firmwares plus a server.
WATER · NATIONAL IRRIGATION AUTHORITY

Smart water metering, leakage detection and irrigation

National Irrigation Authority · 2023 · Lead system designer

Metering, leakage detection and soil sensing across an irrigation scheme, with a microturbine harvesting kinetic energy from the flow to keep the nodes charged. Sensing nodes run ESP32 firmware reporting over MQTT to a Raspberry Pi broker on site, with Node-RED handling local processing.

Before any of it went to the field it was proven on a bench rig of real pipe, valves and pressure gauges — flow is not something you can simulate honestly at a desk. The system was presented publicly in September 2023.

Context
An irrigation scheme loses water it cannot see, and instrumentation in the field has no mains supply.
Work
Smart meters, leakage detection, soil sensing, kinetic microturbine generator. Embedded C/C++, Wi-Fi, MQTT and Node-RED on Raspberry Pi.
Status
Built and demonstrated; presented publicly, September 2023.
